Privacy Policy — ATLAS Inference API

Effective date: 6 July 2026 · Service: atlas.thebeastagi.com

The ATLAS Inference API ("the Service") is an OpenAI-compatible large-language-model inference endpoint operated by ATLAS AGI (Robin Dey, Chiang Mai, Thailand). It serves open-weight models (currently allenai/Olmo-3-7B-Think) on dedicated, single-tenant hardware, primarily to users of aggregation platforms such as OpenRouter.

1. Data we process

When you (or a platform acting on your behalf) send an inference request, the Service processes the request body — including prompts, messages, and sampling parameters — solely to generate the requested completion.

2. Zero retention of prompts and completions

  • Prompt and completion content is processed in memory only and is discarded as soon as the response is delivered.
  • We do not write prompt or completion content to disk, log files, or databases.
  • We do not use your inputs or outputs to train, fine-tune, or evaluate any model.
  • We do not sell, share, or disclose request content to third parties.

3. Operational metadata

For reliability and capacity management, minimal operational metadata (timestamps, HTTP status codes, token counts, error messages) may appear transiently in service logs. These logs never contain request or response content and are periodically rotated and discarded.

4. Infrastructure and subprocessors

  • Google Cloud Platform — hosts the dedicated GPU server (Singapore region, asia-southeast1). Google does not have application-level access to request content.
  • Cloudflare — provides TLS termination and secure tunneling between the public hostname and the origin server. Traffic transits Cloudflare's network subject to Cloudflare's privacy policy; Cloudflare does not retain request bodies on our behalf.

5. Security

All traffic is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+). Inference endpoints require bearer-token authentication. The model runs on single-tenant hardware — no other customers' workloads share the machine.

6. Cookies, analytics, and accounts

The Service is an API. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics or trackers, and maintains no user accounts or profiles.

7. Children

The Service is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly process their personal information.

8. Changes

Updates to this policy will be posted at this URL with a revised effective date.
